# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (v2.1, adopted)

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone. Examples of unacceptable behavior include the use of sexualized
language or imagery, trolling, insulting comments, and publishing others'
private information.

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code may be removed.

This is a research codebase; response times are best-effort.
Full text: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
